How We Keep Directory Data Accurate

No stale information. Vendors broadcast their daily park coordinates straight to our live system.

1. Verified Check-In

Vendors tap "Check-In" on their command center upon putting their truck in park, verifying arrival and service start time.

2. Sold-Out Alerts

When popular specialty cuts run out, trucks mark them unavailable instantly so you never get disappointed in line.

3. Health & Permit Audits

Every listing maintains active municipal food safety licenses and mobile vendor permits verified by our team.
