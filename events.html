Diner Tips

How to Get the Best Festival Experience

Pro-tips to skip lines, stay hydrated, and sample maximum street flavors.

Divide and Conquer

Head to the festival with friends. Each person queues at a different food truck, and everyone meets at the central picnic table to share.

Arrive During Twilight

The golden hour between 5:30 PM and 6:30 PM offers cooler temperatures, ambient twilight lighting, and shorter waiting times before the 8 PM peak rush.

Check Live Sold-Out Status

Open the StreetFeast directory on your mobile browser to view live sold-out badges before walking to the far side of the park.
