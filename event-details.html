Headline Event Saturday, Sep 28 5:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Downtown Sunset Street Feast & Rodeo

Join 4,000+ local foodies at Riverside Promenade for the city’s premier autumn street gathering. 28 verified gourmet trucks, live cumbia and funk bands, craft brewery pavilions, and riverside lounge seating.

The Experience

Everything You Need to Know

The Downtown Sunset Street Feast brings together the most popular trucks in our regional directory. Whether you're craving 14-hour Texas brisket, molten Jalisco birria, wood-fired burrata pizza, or artisanal vegan bowls, every palate is accommodated.

Live Outdoor Stage
Featuring The Austin Latin Brass Collective and DJ Reyna spinning salsa, cumbia & neo-soul.
Craft Beverage Pavilions
Local IPAs, crisp ciders, small-batch hard kombucha, and freshly squeezed agua frescas.

Event Quick Facts

  • Admission: Free for All Ages
  • Pet Policy: Leashed Dogs Welcomed
  • Payment: Cards, Apple Pay, Cash
  • Seating: 120+ Picnic Benches & Lawn
  • Rain Plan: Covered Boardwalk Pavilions

Hour by Hour

Festival Timetable

Plan your evening around music sets, tasting competitions, and kitchen rushes.

5:00 PM Gates Open
Trucks Power Up & DJ Warm-up

Picnic benches open, DJ Reyna kicks off tropical grooves, and first 200 visitors get free street churro tickets.

6:30 PM Live Stage
The Austin Latin Brass Band

High-energy 8-piece brass ensemble covering Latin jazz, salsa, and funk classics as sunset begins over the river.

8:15 PM Showdown
People's Choice Golden Spatula Trophy

Diners cast their digital votes via StreetFeast for best bite of the night. Trophy presented on main stage.

10:30 PM Last Orders
Dessert Afterparty & Festoon Send-off

Last calls for tacos and hot coffee, sweet treats, and festival closing under ambient fireworks.

Getting There

Riverside Promenade Park

Riverside Promenade Park is situated right on the south bank with easy pedestrian, bicycle, and rideshare access.

Designated Event Parking

City Hall Parking Garage (3 blocks away) offers $5 flat event rate. Free street parking after 6 PM.

Rideshare Drop-Off Zone

Set your Uber/Lyft destination to "Riverside East Pier Drop-off" for zero traffic walking entry.

Free Bike Valet

Complimentary secured bicycle parking corrals provided by Austin City Cycling.
