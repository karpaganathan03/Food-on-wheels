Slow-Braised Jalisco Beef Birria, Hand-Pressed Heirloom Corn Tortillas & Fire-Roasted Salsas.

View Live Menu Weekly Spots
Craft & Heritage

7 Years of Searing, Braising & Community Joy

Founded in 2019 by Chef Marco Ruiz, The Taco Wagon began with an antique 1974 trailer and a recipe inherited from Marco's abuela in Guadalajara. Every morning at 4:30 AM, marrow bones, guajillo peppers, cinnamon, and chuck roast simmer slowly for 12 hours into rich, golden-red consomé.

100% Gluten-Free All corn tortillas made fresh
Local Sourcing Grass-fed local Texas beef
